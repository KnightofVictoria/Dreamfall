The Lore

A world built on dreams, forgotten memories, and a little bit of chaos.

✦ The Dreamfall✦ The Star Inn✦ The Portal✦ The War✦ The Creatures

The Dreamfall

Once a peaceful realm, Dreamfall is now fractured — a place where dreams, memories, and reality collide. No one knows exactly how it began or why the world is unraveling.

“Some doors should stay closed. But here, the doors are everywhere.”

The Star Inn

The inn is a strange pocket of safety. Magic woven into its walls prevents weapons and violent objects from being used inside, making it one of the few places where travelers can breathe.

The Portal

A one-way portal lies beyond the inn. Its origin is unknown, and those who enter cannot simply walk back through the same door.

The War

The war left scars across the realm and erased pieces of history. Mylo remembers more about that time than he usually admits.

The Creatures

Animals speak, strange beings wander the forests, and the boundaries between ordinary creatures and magical ones are not always clear.

There is always another door.
