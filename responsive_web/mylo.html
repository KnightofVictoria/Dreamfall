✧ Mylo ✧

♡ About Mylo

Mylo is a talking cat who has lived in Dreamfall for far longer than most people realize. While he may seem like an ordinary cat at first glance, there is much more to him than meets the eye.

Unlike many of the people living in the world after the war, Mylo remembers what came before. His memories make him an important connection to Dreamfall's forgotten past.

✧ Character Details ✧

Name Mylo
Species Cat
Age Unknown
Known For Remembering the world before the war

✧ Personality ✧

Mylo is clever, observant, and difficult to read. He often seems to know more than he says, leaving others to wonder just how much he truly remembers.

Despite his mysterious past, Mylo can be a loyal companion and an important source of guidance.

✧ Story ✧

Mylo predates the war and was involved in the events that shaped Dreamfall. Because of this, his memories may hold pieces of history that have been lost to everyone else.

His connection to the past makes him more than just a companion. He may be one of the few remaining witnesses to what Dreamfall once was.

♡ Mylo's Notes

Mylo has been around much longer than he appears to be. The question is: how much does he actually remember?
