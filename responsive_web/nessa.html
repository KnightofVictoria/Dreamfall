✧ Nessa ✧

♡ About Nessa

Nessa was born after the war, leaving her with no memories of the world that existed before it. Everything she knows about the past comes from stories, fragments, and the people around her.

Growing up in a world shaped by the aftermath of the war, Nessa finds herself surrounded by questions about where she came from and what happened before she was born.

✧ Character Details ✧

Name Nessa
Age Unknown
Origin Dreamfall
Known For Her connection to the post-war world

✧ Personality ✧

Nessa is still discovering who she is and where she belongs in the strange world around her. Her lack of memories makes curiosity an important part of her journey.

✧ Story ✧

Nessa's story is closely connected to the mysteries left behind by the war. As she explores Dreamfall, she begins to encounter pieces of a history that she was never able to experience herself.
