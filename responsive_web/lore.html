Dreamfall Lore

Dreamfall is a world shaped by strange magic, forgotten history, and the stories left behind by those who came before.

The War

A great war changed Dreamfall forever. Much of what happened before and during the conflict has been forgotten or lost.

The Star Inn

The Star Inn is a safe place where magic prevents weapons and violent objects from being used. For many travelers, it provides a rare moment of peace.

The Portal

A mysterious one-way portal exists outside the inn. Its origin is unknown, and no one seems to know where it truly leads.

♡ Lore Notes

Not everything in Dreamfall has an answer. Some mysteries are meant to remain mysterious.
