About the game.

In Dreamland Dungeons you start by choosing your race. There will be many to choose from and from different catagories. You can also combine race options to make a new one. From there you will be taken to a character customization menu where you will have in debth options to change your features. You will be able to change everything from eye color to how long your nails are. If you choose a quadraped race or a race with fur, scales or just multicolor skin, you will be able to customize its color, pattern textureand so forth.
