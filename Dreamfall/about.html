About Dreamfall

A little fantasy. A lot of heart.

✦ ✧ ✦

Why Dreamfall?

Dreamfall is a story about found family, strange worlds, and the courage it takes to keep going — even when you don’t know where you are.

It draws inspiration from whimsical fantasy adventures, dungeon-crawling stories, and quiet fairy tales while building its own world of memories, magic, and chaos.

This is a world where anything can happen. And somehow… it always does.

Creator Note

Dreamfall began as a way to combine the things I love most about fantasy stories: unusual worlds, character creation, talking animals, cozy places, and adventures that can turn strange very quickly.
