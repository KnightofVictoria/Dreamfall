Dreamfall

Dreamfall is a world that never quite learned how to stay in one shape. Once, it was a peaceful realm filled with kingdoms, forests, ancient cities, underground civilizations, and magic that seemed to flow naturally through the land.

Then came the war.

The conflict between the Kingdom of Dreamers and the Dungeon Dwellers changed far more than borders. Magic was twisted, ancient places were disturbed, and something happened in the final days of the war that left the world permanently fractured.

Now, the surface and the depths no longer behave exactly as they once did. Old roads may lead somewhere they shouldn't. Forgotten buildings can appear where there was once nothing but forest. Memories can linger in places long after the people who created them are gone.

Dreamfall is beautiful.

It is also unpredictable.

The Star Inn

Tucked away from the roads and surrounded by a landscape that seems to change depending on who is looking at it, the Star Inn is one of the strangest safe places left in Dreamfall.

From the outside, it looks like an old crooked inn with warm windows, hanging lanterns, and a roof that has seen better centuries. Inside, however, the rules of the world seem to loosen.

Weapons do not work within its walls. Violent magic refuses to take shape. Attempts to harm another person simply fail, as though the inn itself refuses to allow violence through its doors.

Because of this, the Star Inn has become a meeting place for people who would normally never stand in the same room together.

Dreamers, Dungeon Dwellers, travelers, merchants, adventurers, strange creatures, and people who have nowhere else to go can all find a place at its tables.

Nobody agrees on who originally built the Star Inn.

Some believe it existed before the war. Others believe it appeared after the Fracture. A few believe the inn is much older than either kingdom.

Mylo knows more about the Star Inn than he lets on.

The Dream Portal

Somewhere beyond the Star Inn stands a strange portal that nobody can explain.

It does not look like a normal doorway. Its appearance seems to change depending on the time, the weather, and sometimes the person standing in front of it.

What is known is simple:

The portal only goes one way.

Anyone who enters is taken somewhere beyond the known lands of Dreamfall. No traveler has ever returned through the same doorway.

Some claim the portal leads to another part of the world. Others believe it leads somewhere beneath the world. There are even stories that it does not lead to a physical place at all.

Nobody knows who created it.

Nobody knows why it exists.

And most importantly, nobody knows where it sends you.

The Dreaming Forest

The Dreaming Forest stretches across what was once part of the old kingdom's wilderness. It is one of the places where the Fracture seems to have left the deepest mark.

At first glance, the forest appears peaceful. Moss-covered trees, glowing flowers, quiet streams, and soft paths create an almost storybook atmosphere.

But the forest has never been entirely ordinary.

Paths can move.

Trees sometimes grow in places they could not have reached naturally. Familiar landmarks may disappear for days and then return exactly where they were.

Some travelers have reported hearing voices belonging to people they have never met. Others claim they have seen memories from the past appearing between the trees like scenes from a forgotten dream.

The forest is not necessarily hostile.

It simply does not always understand the difference between what happened, what is happening, and what someone wishes had happened.

The Dungeons

Beneath Dreamfall lies another world entirely.

The dungeons are not simply collections of monsters waiting for adventurers. They are enormous underground environments containing cities, villages, ruins, markets, temples, libraries, farms, and civilizations that have existed for generations.

The people who live below the surface are known collectively as the Dungeon Dwellers, although they are not one single species or culture.

Some settlements have never seen the surface. Others remember a time when trade between the two kingdoms was possible.

Deeper levels of the dungeons become increasingly difficult to understand. Ancient structures appear to have been carved into the world long before either kingdom existed.

The deepest places are especially dangerous.

They are also where the oldest secrets of Dreamfall may still be buried.

The Forgotten Ruins

Across Dreamfall are the remains of the world that existed before the war.

Castles stand empty beneath forests. Old roads disappear beneath overgrown gardens. Villages remain frozen in strange moments of time.

Some ruins are completely abandoned.

Others are not.

Because of the Fracture, certain locations appear to remember what happened within them. A traveler may enter an empty ballroom and hear music from a celebration that happened centuries ago. Another ruin may briefly appear exactly as it did before the war.

These places are valuable to historians, treasure hunters, adventurers, and anyone searching for information about the old kingdoms.

Unfortunately, the past does not always appreciate being disturbed.

Places Unknown

Not every place in Dreamfall has a name.

There are locations that appear only during certain nights. Doors that lead somewhere different every time they are opened. Staircases that descend farther than the buildings containing them should allow.

Some places may belong to the surface.

Some may belong to the dungeons.

Others may not belong to either.

The Fracture made it difficult to know where the boundaries of the world truly end. Maps can become outdated overnight, and stories about distant locations often contradict one another.

For travelers, this makes exploration dangerous.

For adventurers, it makes exploration irresistible.

Every dungeon has a bottom. Every dream has an end.

The question is what happens when you reach either.

Every path leads somewhere. Not every path leads back.
